# YuNet timeline fixture

`yunet-faces.mp4` has sixteen 320 × 240 frames at 24 fps and a silent stereo AAC track. Frames 0–4 and 10–15 show the reference portrait with a moving, rotating crop. Both faces appear in frames 5–6. Only the second face appears in frames 7–8. Frame 9 is blank. These frame numbers are zero-based.

The fixture uses crops from two public-domain White House photographs by Pete Souza and Eric Draper. [Reference portrait](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:President_Barack_Obama.jpg) · [Second portrait](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:George-W-Bush.jpeg)
